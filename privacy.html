Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-11.5 · Published by Eyinsan Odugba

About this policy

Effective 11 November 2026 (version 2026-11-11.5). This policy explains what happens to information when you use 54Scholars. It is written to meet the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the data protection laws of other African countries where the app is used, including the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act 2013 and Ghana's Data Protection Act 2012.

In short

We keep no one's data. Everything about you stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no ad networks, no analytics, no tracking and no data brokers, and nothing is sold or shared for advertising. Posts and messages you choose to send us carry no name, account, email, phone or device ID. Sponsored listings are always labelled.

Who is responsible

The app is published by Eyinsan Odugba, the developer of 54Scholars. Your information stays on your phone. The only things that reach our systems are posts and messages you choose to send us, which carry no name, account, email, phone or device ID (see Posts and messages you send us). For any privacy question, request or complaint, contact our data protection contact at the email shown on our App Store and Google Play pages.

What stays on your phone

Your profile answers (nationality, study level, field and any optional details you add, such as birth year), the scholarships you save, skip or follow, your notes, application status, planner ticks, referees, CV, cover letters, essays, answers and settings. They are kept on your phone, encrypted, and used only by the app on your phone to show you matches, reminders and tools. You can see and change them in the app, and erase them at any time.

What you must give, and what is optional

No law requires you to give us anything. To show matches, the app asks for your nationality and study level; without them it can only show the full list. Everything else (field, birth year, results, CV and the rest) is optional: if you skip it, matches are simply less exact.

The legal basis

The app uses your information only on your own phone, at your request and under your control. When you choose to send us a post or a message, our basis is your consent (you decide to send it, and you can delete it at any time with Erase everything) and our legitimate interest in keeping posts safe and answering you.

Your CV, letters and essays

A CV, cover letter, essay or answer you write in the app, and any old CV you bring in, stays on your phone, encrypted with the rest of your data. PDFs, Word files and plan exports are made on your phone, and a file you pick is read on your phone. We never see them unless you choose to send them to someone yourself. Referees you add stay on your phone and the app never contacts them; ask their permission first.

Notifications

If you allow them, deadline reminders are scheduled on your phone. They are not sent through our servers, and we cannot see them.

Purchases

If you buy Plus, Apple or Google takes the payment and handles your account, payment details and receipts under their own privacy policies; they are independent controllers of that information. We never see your name, email or card. The app checks the purchase on your phone, and the store restores it on a new phone.

Sponsored cards

When a sponsored card appears, your phone chose it using the profile stored on your phone. Nothing about you is sent to us or to the sponsor, and sponsors never learn who saw or tapped their card.

Sharing and links

When you share a scholarship, your phone's own share menu sends its name, deadline and official link, with an invitation to get 54Scholars. Nothing about you is added. Opening a listing takes you to the funder's own website, which has its own privacy policy. Our own web pages (such as this policy) use no cookies, analytics or trackers.

What reaches us

Nothing about you, unless you choose to send something. To bring you new and corrected scholarships and approved community posts, your phone downloads public lists from our website and our server. These downloads send no name, account, device ID, advertising ID or cookie, and nothing you entered in the app. Like any website, the host briefly sees the internet address a request comes from, as it must to deliver it; we do not keep logs that link a download to you. The scholarship list is digitally signed, and your phone ignores any list that we did not sign.

Posts and messages you send us

When you send a success story, an experience with an agent, a scam report, a scholarship suggestion or a message to our team, the app sends only what you wrote, with no name, account, email, phone, device ID or location. Messages that contain your own email address or phone number are refused, so please do not write personal details. Our server keeps what you sent, its status, the day it arrived and, for each item, a scrambled copy of a random code that only your phone holds; the code lets our answer come back to your phone and nobody else's. Our server does not store internet addresses: it uses them only for a few minutes, in memory, to stop flooding. Every post is read by our team before anything is published, and posts are published without your name. Our team may use an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude) to help check posts against the posting rules and to help prepare answers; it sees only what you wrote, never anything that identifies you, and a person stays responsible for every decision. How long we keep it: posts we do not publish, 30 days; answered messages, 90 days after we answer; handled scam reports and suggestions, 90 days; anything not reviewed, 180 days; published posts, until they are removed. Erase everything in Me deletes everything this phone sent, on our side too.

Agents who contact us

If you are an agent (a business) and register or send a reply or appeal in the app, the app opens your own email app with a message to us, and nothing is sent unless you press Send. It contains only what you typed: the name you work under, country, the contact details and business details you chose to give, and your reply. You give these details so that we can check you with official sources and, for registrations, show them on your listing. We use them only for that, keep the email only until your listing or reply is decided (and while a listing based on it is shown), and delete it when you ask. Your email provider handles the message under its own policy.

If you email us

If you choose Email us in the app, or write to our address yourself, your own email app sends us your message and your email address, and nothing is sent unless you press Send. We use them only to answer you. We keep the email until your question is answered and for up to 90 days after, and delete it sooner when you ask. Your email provider handles the message under its own policy.

Transfers abroad

Your profile and saved information never leave your phone. Posts and messages you send us are stored by our server provider, DigitalOcean, in the data centre region we choose (the United Kingdom or the European Union), and an AI assistant run by Anthropic in the United States may help review them; both are bound by contracts that protect the data. Apple and Google may process purchase information in other countries under their own policies and safeguards. Our website host and the email provider that receives emails from agents may handle internet addresses and those emails in other countries under their own safeguards.

How long information is kept

Information on your phone is kept until you delete it, use Erase everything in Me, or uninstall the app. We keep nothing about you. Posts and messages you choose to send us are kept for the times set out in Posts and messages you send us, an email that an agent chooses to send us as set out in Agents who contact us, and an email you choose to send us as set out in If you email us. If you lose or reset your phone, we cannot restore your information, because we never had it.

Security

What you save is encrypted on your phone with a key that only that phone holds. You can add an app lock and hide the app in the app switcher. Erase everything deletes your data and the key, and asks our server to delete what this phone sent. The app opens only official https links. Our server accepts only encrypted connections, keeps no internet addresses, and only our team can read the review queue.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and similar laws, you have the right to be informed; to access, correct and delete your data; to restrict or object to its use; to move it; to withdraw consent; and not to be subject to decisions made only by automated means that seriously affect you. Because your data is on your phone, you can do most of this yourself at any time in the app. Matches and scores are suggestions to help you; they never decide anything about you. We will answer any request free of charge within 30 days, or sooner where your law requires.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle privacy, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority, for example the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng), Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke), South Africa's Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za) or Ghana's Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.org.gh).

Children

54Scholars is for ages 13 and over; under-13s see a kind message and nothing is saved. We collect no one's data, children included: your age answer and profile stay on your phone. If you are under 18, the app turns off public posting, sponsored cards and purchases, and we never profile, advertise to or market to you. You can still write to our team privately (no name or contact details needed), so anyone who needs help can reach us.

If something goes wrong

We hold no data that identifies you. Our server holds only anonymous posts and messages, without names, contact details, device IDs or internet addresses. If a security problem ever affects the app, our server or the lists the app downloads, we will fix it, tell you without delay, and notify the relevant data protection authorities within the time the law requires (72 hours in Nigeria and Kenya, sooner where required).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app shows the new version and asks you to agree again. We will never start collecting your personal data without first changing this policy and, where the law requires, asking for your consent.

Contact

Privacy questions, requests and complaints: contact Eyinsan Odugba, the developer of 54Scholars at the email shown on our App Store and Google Play pages.
